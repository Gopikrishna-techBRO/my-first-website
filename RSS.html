🪷 The Path of Bhakti

Krishna consciousness teaches that the ultimate purpose of life is to understand our spiritual identity and develop loving devotion toward the Supreme Lord.

Radha–Shyāmasundara

In the Vaishnava tradition, Krishna is understood as the Supreme Personality of Godhead, and Srimati Radharani is revered as His supreme devotee and divine counterpart.


Devotees cultivate remembrance of Krishna through chanting, hearing, worship, studying scripture and serving others.

📖 Foundations of ISKCON Philosophy

These are some important ideas within the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition followed by ISKCON.

🕉️

Our Spiritual Identity

The living being is understood as an eternal spiritual soul, distinct from the temporary physical body.

💙

Krishna

Krishna is understood as the Supreme Personality of Godhead and the ultimate source of all existence.

📿

Bhakti-Yoga

Bhakti-yoga is the path of developing loving devotion through practices such as chanting, hearing and service.

🌱

Karma & Rebirth

Vedic philosophy explains that actions have consequences and that the soul experiences repeated birth and death until spiritual liberation.

📚

Bhagavad-gītā

The Bhagavad-gītā presents teachings on the soul, duty, yoga, devotion and the relationship between the individual and Krishna.

❤️

Seva

Spiritual life includes service. Devotees try to use their abilities, time and resources in service to Krishna and others.

"The goal of spiritual life is to awaken our natural relationship with the Supreme and develop genuine devotion."
— Krishna-consciousness principle

📜 Bhagavad-gītā

The Bhagavad-gītā is a dialogue between Krishna and Arjuna on the battlefield of Kurukshetra.

🧘

Self-Realization

Understand the difference between the temporary body and the eternal self.

⚔️

Dharma

Learn how to perform one's responsibilities while developing spiritual consciousness.

🙏

Devotion

Krishna teaches the importance of surrender and loving devotional service.

📿 Hare Krishna Mahā-mantra

Chanting the Hare Krishna mantra is a central spiritual practice in ISKCON.

Hare Krishna Hare Krishna
Krishna Krishna Hare Hare
Hare Rama Hare Rama
Rama Rama Hare Hare

The mantra is traditionally chanted as a meditation and devotional practice.

🌱 Four Regulative Principles

ISKCON devotees traditionally follow four regulative principles as part of their spiritual discipline.

1

No meat, fish or eggs

Devotees follow a vegetarian diet and traditionally offer food to Krishna.

2

No intoxication

Avoiding intoxicating substances supports clarity and spiritual discipline.

3

No gambling

Devotees avoid gambling as part of cultivating a disciplined lifestyle.

4

No illicit sexual activity

Devotees follow traditional principles of responsible and disciplined family life.

📚 Srila Prabhupada


His Divine Grace A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada founded the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON) in 1966.


He translated and commented on important Vaishnava texts and established temples, communities and educational programs dedicated to spreading Krishna consciousness.
